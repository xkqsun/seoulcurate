Programs

The 3-Day Intensive Reset is designed for executives and professionals who cannot step away for extended periods. It focuses on rapid biomarker assessment, targeted interventions for sleep, stress, and metabolic function, and a compressed but clinically rigorous protocol. You leave with a 90-day health blueprint.

The 5-Day Transformation Journey goes deeper — it includes advanced genomic and microbiome analysis, daily physician-guided sessions, personalized nutrition protocols, and extended recovery and integration time. Clinical outcomes are measurably stronger at the 90-day follow-up. We recommend the 5-Day program for participants seeking lasting physiological change.

94% of 5-Day participants report measurable improvement at 90-day follow-up

Each day is individually structured based on your baseline assessments, but a typical day includes:

  • 6:30 AM — Morning biomarker check-in (HRV, cortisol, glucose)
  • 7:00 AM — Physician-guided movement session (breathwork, mobility, low-intensity cardio)
  • 8:00 AM — Precision nutrition breakfast with clinical dietitian
  • 9:30 AM — One-on-one physician consultation and protocol review
  • 11:00 AM–1:00 PM — Therapeutic interventions (IV nutrition, photobiomodulation, cold therapy, etc.) based on your protocol
  • 2:00 PM — Guided rest, meditation, or nature immersion
  • 4:00 PM — Group learning session or biofeedback training
  • 6:30 PM — Therapeutic dinner with clinical nutrition guidance
  • 8:00 PM — Evening wind-down protocol, sleep optimization preparation

Schedules are adjusted daily based on your body's response to interventions.

We strongly recommend digital minimalism during the retreat — not as a restriction, but because cortisol regulation (a core therapeutic goal) is directly impaired by reactive communication patterns. Our clinical data shows participants who disconnect fully achieve 40% better outcomes on stress biomarkers.

That said, we understand the reality of executive life. We offer a structured connectivity window from 12:30–1:30 PM daily, with a dedicated business center for urgent matters. Our concierge team can also manage communications on your behalf. Many of our clients find this the first genuine disconnect from reactive work they have experienced in years.

Post-retreat support is integral to our protocol, not an add-on. Every participant receives:

  • A personalized 90-Day Health Blueprint with daily protocols, nutrition guidance, and supplement recommendations
  • Physician check-in calls at 2 weeks, 6 weeks, and 90 days post-retreat
  • Access to your clinical data and biomarker trends via our secure patient portal
  • Priority scheduling for a follow-up retreat at a discounted rate
  • Access to our monthly virtual alumni sessions (Q&A with clinical team)

The retreat is the reset. The 90-day protocol is the transformation.

Medical & Safety

Our clinical team includes board-certified physicians across internal medicine, functional and integrative medicine, occupational medicine, lifestyle medicine, cardiology, and behavioral health. All physicians are active practitioners with affiliations at leading academic medical centers including Johns Hopkins, UCSF, NYU Langone, and Harvard.

Every retreat is staffed with a minimum of one attending physician per eight participants, a registered dietitian, a licensed behavioral health clinician, and certified wellness practitioners. We maintain the same staffing ratios as accredited outpatient clinical programs.

Prior to your retreat, our medical team conducts a comprehensive pre-retreat health assessment, including a review of your medical history, current medications, recent labs, and health goals. Your program is then individually designed around your clinical profile. No intervention is administered without physician review and your informed consent.

We do maintain a small number of absolute contraindications (active malignancy, certain cardiac arrhythmias, etc.). These are reviewed during your pre-retreat assessment. If we cannot safely accommodate you, we will refer you to appropriate care.

Pricing & Booking

All programs are fully all-inclusive. The price covers:

  • All medical consultations, assessments, and clinical interventions
  • Comprehensive lab work (biomarker panel, hormones, inflammation markers, metabolic panel)
  • Private accommodation at our retreat facility
  • All meals, designed and supervised by our clinical dietitian
  • Therapeutic sessions (IV nutrition, cold/heat therapy, photobiomodulation, biofeedback, etc.)
  • Post-retreat 90-Day Health Blueprint and follow-up physician calls
  • Access to alumni portal and monthly clinical Q&A sessions

The only items not included are travel to and from the retreat location and any additional advanced genomic testing you opt into (add-on, clearly priced).

HSA/FSA: Many of our medical services qualify as eligible HSA/FSA expenses. We provide itemized receipts that separate medical services from accommodation costs to facilitate HSA/FSA claims. We recommend confirming eligibility with your benefits administrator, as rules vary by plan.

Insurance reimbursement: We are not in-network with insurance providers, but we provide detailed superbills (itemized clinical documentation) that some participants successfully submit for out-of-network reimbursement for specific medical services. Our team can advise on the documentation process.

Corporate billing: For corporate programs, we can structure invoicing as an employee wellness benefit, training and development expense, or executive health program — depending on how your organization categorizes wellness spending.

We understand that executive schedules change. Our policy:

  • 60+ days before retreat: Full refund or credit (no fee)
  • 30–59 days before retreat: 50% refund or full credit toward a future retreat
  • 14–29 days before retreat: Credit only (no refund), valid for 18 months
  • Under 14 days: Non-refundable, but we will do our best to accommodate rescheduling based on availability

All cancellations and reschedules are processed without penalty up to 72 hours after booking (cooling-off period). For medical emergencies or force majeure, we handle situations individually with full discretion.

Corporate Programs

Our standard corporate cohort programs begin at 8 participants. This minimum ensures your team benefits from both the individual clinical experience and the group dynamic that reinforces behavioral change post-retreat.

For organizations wishing to send 2–7 participants, we can integrate them into our standard cohort schedule alongside other participants (typically executives from non-competing industries). For 20+ participants, we offer fully private retreat buyouts at our facilities.

We provide your HR and benefits team with a Corporate Outcomes Report at 30, 60, and 90 days post-retreat. This report includes aggregate, fully de-identified data on:

  • Cohort-level biomarker improvements (stress hormones, inflammatory markers, sleep quality)
  • Self-reported productivity scores (standardized validated scale)
  • Absence and sick-day utilization (if you share HR data)
  • Re-enrollment intent and Net Promoter Score

Our clients have reported measurable outcomes including reduced sick days, lower healthcare claims, and improved performance reviews — but we present what the data shows for your specific cohort, not industry averages.

Logistics

Our name reflects our ethos: we are deliberately accessible from major urban centers. All 8 locations are within 90 minutes of a major international airport, so you spend minimal time traveling and maximum time recovering. Current locations span the US, UK, Singapore, and UAE, with European locations opening in 2026.

Each facility is purpose-built or extensively renovated to meet clinical standards while maintaining the aesthetic and comfort of a premium private hotel. Specific location details are provided upon booking confirmation.

Three weeks before your retreat, you'll receive a comprehensive Pre-Retreat Preparation Guide and complete your medical intake. In terms of packing:

  • Comfortable, breathable clothing for movement sessions (athletic wear)
  • Any prescription medications in original packaging (our pharmacist will review)
  • A journal — many participants find this retreat catalyzes clarity worth capturing
  • Formal or smart-casual attire is entirely optional; our facilities are informal

Everything else — toiletries, supplements, clinical supplies, nutrition — is provided. We also recommend reducing caffeine intake gradually in the two weeks before arrival to minimize withdrawal effects during your cortisol reset protocol.

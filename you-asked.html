A recovery guide from someone who's had it done. Real questions people search for, answered with what clinics commonly advise and how I'd spend those days in Seoul.

这是一份由亲身经历过的人写的恢复指南。针对大家真实搜索的问题，结合诊所通常给出的建议，以及我会如何在首尔度过那几天来回答。

For about the first week your skin is still healing, so clinics commonly ask you to avoid:
  • Heat: saunas, hot baths, workouts that make you sweat
  • Actives: retinol, vitamin C, AHA/BHA, scrubs and exfoliants
  • Friction: wash gently with foam, no rubbing, and never pick at scabs
  • Alcohol and smoking
In Seoul, that means saving the jjimjilbang and spa saunas for later. I swap them for a comfortable lounge or a slow meal.
术后大约一周内皮肤仍在恢复，诊所通常会建议避免：

  • 刺激：视黄醇、维生素C、AHA/BHA等功效成分，磨砂和去角质
  • 摩擦：洗脸时用泡沫轻柔清洗，不要揉搓，结痂不要强行撕掉
  • 饮酒和吸烟
在首尔，这意味着汗蒸房和水疗桑拿要留到以后。我会换成舒适的休息空间，或一顿慢慢吃的饭。

It depends on the treatment, so I plan by downtime, not by treatment name: same day to 1 day, 2–4 days, or a week or more. Your clinic will tell you which one you're in. Recovery usually moves like this:
  • Days 1–2: redness, heat and swelling peak. Focus on moisture and sun protection
  • Days 3–7: flaking or small scabs form and fall off. Itchy, but don't pick. Keep moisturizing
  • Weeks 1–2: it looks healed, but skin is still recovering underneath. Bring actives back slowly, as your clinic advises

因治疗项目而异，所以我不是按治疗名称，而是按恢复期来安排：当天至1天、2～4天、一周以上，共三个阶段。你属于哪一个，诊所会告诉你。恢复通常是这样的：
  • 第1～2天：泛红、发热、肿胀最明显的时期。专注保湿和防晒
  • 第3～7天：出现脱皮或小结痂并脱落的时期。即使发痒也不要抠，继续保湿
  • 第1～2周：表面看起来已经好了，但内部仍在恢复。功效型护肤品请按诊所建议慢慢恢复使用

Many people are told to go easy on alcohol and very salty or spicy food, which can make swelling worse. Instead, fill up on what helps skin repair:
  • Protein: eggs, tofu, chicken, fish
  • Vitamin C–rich fruit and vegetables: strawberries, kiwi, broccoli
  • Omega-3: salmon, mackerel, walnuts
  • Plenty of water
Seoul is very good at gentle food like this: rice porridge (juk), clear soups, grilled fish, tofu dishes, temple-style vegetables. That's how I plan meals during recovery.
很多人会被建议少喝酒，少吃太咸或太辣的食物，因为可能加重肿胀。取而代之，多吃有助于皮肤修复的食物：

首尔很擅长这类温和的食物：粥、清汤、烤鱼、豆腐料理、寺庙素食。恢复期的饮食，我主要按这些来安排。
